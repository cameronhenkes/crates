## The module, updated in the worktree

In `~/Projects/career/portfolio/portfolio-crate-home` (branch `redesign/crate-records`),
`src/components/CrateFold3D/crate-fold.js` and `crate-fold.d.ts` are updated and UNCOMMITTED.
Both were unchanged since the portfolio's last commit of them. They typecheck and lint clean.

The addition: option `brightness` (default 1) and method `setBrightness(value)`.

Cameron found the 3D crate too dark on a white page and fine on a dark one. It was lit for a
dark page. Use 1 on the dark theme and 2 on the light theme, and call `setBrightness` when the
theme switches. At 2 the lit faces match the flat icon's colour: the foot rail measures
(185, 96, 75) against the icon's (196, 101, 79), with no clipping. This applies to every crate
on the site, including the home shelf.
